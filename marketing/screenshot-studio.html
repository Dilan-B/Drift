DRIFT / SCREENSHOT STUDIO
APP STORE · 6.9" · 1290 × 2796
How to use. Click any frame to drop in a raw screenshot (or drag one onto it). Edit the kicker and headline directly in the card. Toggle light/dark per frame to match the screenshot you dropped in — a dark-mode capture on a cream frame looks broken. Export writes true 1290 × 2796 PNGs, which is Apple's 6.9" requirement; the preview is the same render pipeline at 0.2 scale, so what you see is exactly what exports.

Order matters. Frames 1–3 are what most people ever see. Lead with the shield (tension), then the unlock (payoff), then family mode (breadth).

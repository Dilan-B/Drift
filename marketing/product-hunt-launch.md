# Product Hunt Launch — Drift

## Tagline (60 chars max)
Earn your screen time by completing real tasks

## Description
Drift blocks your distracting apps (TikTok, Instagram, games) until you complete real-world tasks — homework, exercise, chores, anything meaningful. Complete a task, take a photo to prove it, and AI verifies your work. Then your apps unlock for the time you earned.

Built by two high school students (Riaan and Dilan) who were tired of their own doom-scrolling habits. No ads, no tracking, no premium tier. Just a fair system that turns screen time from a guilty habit into a reward for getting things done.

**How it works:**
1. Set tasks you want to accomplish today
2. Complete them and submit proof (photo or description)
3. AI verifies the task is actually done
4. Earn screen-time minutes that unlock your blocked apps
5. Run out? Do another task

**Key features:**
- Real app blocking via Apple Screen Time API (can't just tap "Ignore")
- AI-verified task completion (no cheating)
- Calendar integration — your assignments become tasks
- Family mode — parents set tasks, kids earn time
- Referral system — invite friends, both get bonus time
- Location-based task suggestions
- Daily streaks and progress tracking

**Built with:**
- Expo / React Native
- Supabase (auth, database, edge functions)
- OpenAI (task verification)
- Apple FamilyControls / ManagedSettings

Free on the App Store. No catch.

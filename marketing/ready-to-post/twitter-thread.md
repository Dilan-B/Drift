**Thread:**

1/ I'm 17 and I built an app that blocks TikTok until you finish your homework.

Here's why screen time limits don't work, and what does 🧵

2/ The average teen spends 7+ hours a day on their phone. I was one of them.

I tried every solution:
- Screen time limits → tapped "Ignore"
- Deleted apps → re-downloaded by lunch
- Willpower → lasted until 2 PM

Nothing worked because they all rely on self-control you don't have at 9 PM.

3/ So my friend @dilan and I tried something different.

Instead of LIMITING screen time, we made it something you EARN.

Complete a real task → get X minutes of phone time.
No task → apps stay locked.

Simple.

4/ We built @daboringapp (Drift) to automate this:

- Set your tasks (homework, exercise, chores)
- Complete them + submit photo proof
- AI verifies you actually did it
- Your apps unlock for the time you earned

Uses Apple's Screen Time API so you can't cheat.

5/ The psychology behind it:

"I can't use my phone" = deprivation → resistance
"I can use my phone after I finish this" = motivation → action

You're not fighting willpower. You're channeling it.

6/ Results after 1 year:
- Screen time: 6h → 2h daily
- GPA: up a full point
- Phone arguments with parents: zero
- Tasks completed: hundreds

Not because I'm more disciplined. The system just makes productivity the easier path.

We built it for ourselves and figured other people might want it too.

iPhone only for now (Apple's Screen Time API doesn't exist on Android yet).

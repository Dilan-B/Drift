# Screen recordings wanted

Drop them in this folder (`public/raw/`). Any length, unedited, don't bother
trimming — I crop to 1080x1920, cut, and speed-adjust.

**Check each one before you drop it**: notification banners, real names,
other people's usernames. These go into public videos.

---

## Priority 1 — unlocks the most

**1. Hitting the shield** · ~8s
Open a blocked app from the home screen, get the Screen Time shield, back out.
Do it twice in the clip if it's quick — the repetition reads as frustration.
*Unlocks: screen-demo, pov. This is the single most valuable clip.*

**2. Completing a task, minutes landing** · ~10s
Tick a task, take the proof photo, AI verifies, the earned minutes go up.
The number changing on screen is the payoff — hold on it for a beat.
*Unlocks: screen-demo, before-after.*

**3. Timer running down** · ~6s
Earned time counting down while an app is open. Ideally catch the moment it
runs out and the shield comes back.
*Unlocks: pov, before-after.*

## Priority 2 — makes videos better, not longer

**4. Task list, real tasks** · ~6s
Scroll the Today list with believable tasks on it. Not "test" — homework,
gym, dishes. A few done, a few not.

**5. Adding a task** · ~8s
Tap Add task, type it, set the reward, save. Shows how little friction there is.

**6. iOS Screen Time report** · 2 screenshots
Settings → Screen Time. One from a bad week, one from a good one. This is the
whole before-after format and it's two screenshots, not a recording.

## Priority 3 — one format, but a strong one

**7. Studying with the phone locked** · 20-30s
Filmed, not screen-recorded. Phone face down or across the desk, you actually
working, natural light, no talking. Slow and calm.
*Unlocks: study-with-me — companion content is pulling hard right now and it's
the one format where Drift is part of the thing rather than advertised during it.*

If you only do three: **1, 2 and 6.**

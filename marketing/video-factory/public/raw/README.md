# Drop raw phone captures here

Screen recordings and screenshots straight off the phone — any size, any
length, unedited. Nothing in this folder is used directly.

I trim, crop to 1080x1920, speed-adjust and denoise them into `public/capture/`,
which is what the pipeline actually reads.

Before dropping anything in, check the recording for: notification banners,
your real name, contacts, or anything else personal that the status bar or a
pull-down might have caught. These end up in public videos.

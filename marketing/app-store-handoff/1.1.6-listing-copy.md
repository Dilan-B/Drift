## What's New in This Version

4000 character limit. **Requires review to change.**

```
Siri support
Ask Siri to check your balance, create a task, or start a Drift In session. Nothing to set up. The same commands work in Shortcuts, so you can automate them however you like.

A block screen that knows what it's blocking
Instead of a generic message, the screen now names the app you're reaching for and what it takes to unlock it.

Share your wins
Finish a task and you can share what you earned.

A check-in when you're deep in a scroll
If you've been spending earned time for a while, Drift quietly asks whether it's still what you want. Never more than a few times a day.

Fixes
• Tasks imported from your calendar now go through the same AI check as every other task. Before, they unlocked with a single tap.
• Fixed a bug that signed some people out at random.
• Buttons now respond with haptic feedback, plus small polish throughout.
```

No em dashes, matching the house preference applied to the notification copy.

## Before you submit

**The Siri claim is unverified.** The App Intents code compiles and the utterances
are registered, but no one has said the phrases to a real device. App Review does
test feature claims made in What's New. If a reviewer says "Hey Siri, check my
Drift balance" and nothing happens, that is a rejection and costs days.

Test on TestFlight first:

1. Say all four phrases — balance, create task, start session, today's progress.
2. Open a blocked app and confirm the shield names it without truncating.
3. Confirm the new onboarding slide (slide 5) renders.

**If you can't test first**, use this safer What's New instead — it leads with the
block screen and holds the Siri announcement for 1.1.7:

```
A block screen that knows what it's blocking
Instead of a generic message, the screen now names the app you're reaching for and what it takes to unlock it.

Share your wins
Finish a task and you can share what you earned.

A check-in when you're deep in a scroll
If you've been spending earned time for a while, Drift quietly asks whether it's still what you want. Never more than a few times a day.

Fixes
• Tasks imported from your calendar now go through the same AI check as every other task. Before, they unlocked with a single tap.
• Fixed a bug that signed some people out at random.
• Buttons now respond with haptic feedback, plus small polish throughout.
```

And swap the promotional text to:

```
Blocked apps stay blocked until you earn them back. Complete a real task, verified by AI, and unlock your screen time. Free, no ads, no tracking.
```

## Other 1.1.6 notes

- **Minimum iOS is now 17.0** (was 15.1). Apple surfaces this on the listing
  automatically; it does not need mentioning in What's New.
- **Export compliance** is now declared in Info.plist, so builds compiled after
  `6337d4a` will not show "Missing Compliance". Builds 97, 99 and 100 predate it
  and still need the question answered by hand.

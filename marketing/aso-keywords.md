## App Store Description

**Earn your screen time. Don't just scroll — achieve.**

Drift flips screen time on its head. Instead of mindlessly scrolling, you complete real tasks to earn minutes of app access. When your time runs out, distracting apps are blocked until you earn more.

**How it works:**
• Set up tasks — homework, exercise, chores, reading, or anything meaningful
• Complete tasks and earn screen-time minutes
• AI verifies your work with photos or text — no cheating
• Blocked apps unlock when you've earned enough time
• Run out? Complete more tasks to earn more

**Built for students, by students:**
Drift was built by two teens who were tired of their own doom-scrolling habits. It uses Apple's Screen Time API to actually block apps — not just send reminders you'll ignore.

**Features:**
• AI-verified task completion (photo + text proof)
• Real app blocking via Apple Screen Time
• Daily streaks and progress tracking
• Calendar integration — turn events into tasks
• Location-based task suggestions
• Friends and accountability partners
• Shield themes to customize your lock screen
• The Lab — experimental productivity features
• Works for individuals and families

**Privacy first:**
Your data stays on your device. Task photos are analyzed by AI and never stored. Contact matching uses encrypted hashes — emails never leave your phone.

Free to use. No ads. No hidden fees.

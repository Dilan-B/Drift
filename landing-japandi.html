Our philosophy

Screen time isn't the enemy. Mindless screen time is.

Most apps try to block you from your phone. Drift gives you a reason to put it down. Complete real tasks, earn real time. The balance is yours to spend however you choose.

Earned, not restricted

Every minute of screen time comes from something you accomplished. A workout, a chapter read, a room tidied. The reward feels different when you've earned it.

Honest accountability

The timer only drains while you're using blocked apps. Switch to something else, and it pauses. No tricks, no hidden penalties.

Your pace, your rules

Set your own difficulty. Choose your tasks. Pick which apps to block. Drift adapts to you, not the other way around.

The method

Three quiet steps between you and a more intentional relationship with your phone.

01

Choose what to block

Select the apps that take more than they give. TikTok, Instagram, X, games — whatever pulls you in. Drift uses Apple's Screen Time to shield them.

02

Complete real tasks

Pick from suggested tasks or create your own. Work out, read, study, meditate. Each one earns minutes of screen time based on your difficulty setting.

03

Spend what you've earned

Your balance counts down only while blocked apps are open. When it reaches zero, the shield returns. Tomorrow, you begin again.

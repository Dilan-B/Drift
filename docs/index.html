How it works

Four steps to
guilt-free scrolling

✏️

Create tasks

Add the things you actually need to do — homework, chores, exercise, reading. Set how many minutes each one is worth.

✅

Complete them

Do the work, mark it done. Optionally snap a photo for AI verification — prove it, don't just check it off.

⏱️

Earn minutes

Each completed task adds screen time to your daily balance. The more you do, the more you unlock.

📱

Use your apps

Your blocked apps unlock as you earn time. When your balance hits zero, they're blocked again — by iOS itself.

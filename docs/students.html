Stop Doom-Scrolling. Start Earning.

The productivity app built by students who get it

You open your phone to check one thing. An hour later you're deep in TikTok and your homework isn't done. Sound familiar? You're not lazy — your phone is literally designed to keep you scrolling.

Drift fixes this by making screen time something you earn.

Finish your homework → earn 30 minutes of TikTok. Go for a run → earn 20 minutes of Instagram. It's that simple.

How Students Use Drift

  • Before class: Set your tasks for the day — assignments, reading, workout, whatever you need to get done.
  • During the day: Complete tasks and snap a photo as proof. AI verifies it's legit.
  • Earned your time? Your apps unlock. Scroll guilt-free because you actually did the work.
  • Run out? Do another task. It's a game you win by being productive.

Why This Works Better Than Willpower

You've tried deleting apps. You've tried screen time limits. You turned them off the same day. Drift is different because it doesn't rely on willpower — it creates a system where productivity and screen time work together instead of against each other.

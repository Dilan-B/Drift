The Screen Time App That Ends the Argument

For parents who are tired of fighting about phones

You've tried taking the phone away. You've tried screen time limits. You've tried the "one more hour" negotiation. None of it works because it's all based on restriction — and kids push back against restriction.

Drift takes a different approach: your kids earn their screen time by completing tasks you set.

Instead of "you can't have your phone," the conversation becomes "here's how you earn your phone." It's fair, it's clear, and it works.

How It Works for Families

1

You set the tasks

Homework, chores, reading, practice — anything meaningful. Set how many minutes of screen time each task is worth.

2

Your kid completes tasks

They take a photo or describe what they did. AI verifies it's real — no faking a clean room with yesterday's photo.

3

They earn screen time

Completed tasks unlock minutes of app access. When time runs out, apps are blocked again until they earn more.

Why Kids Actually Like It

This sounds like it would start another fight, but the opposite happens. Kids respond well to systems that feel fair. When the rules are clear and they have agency ("I can earn more time whenever I want"), the resentment disappears.

"My son actually asked me to add more tasks so he could earn more time. That has never happened with any other approach."

What Makes Drift Different

  • Real app blocking — Uses Apple's Screen Time API. Kids can't just open Settings and turn it off.
  • AI verification — Tasks are verified with photos. No more "I already did it, trust me."
  • Family accounts — Set up a parent account and link your child's device. You set tasks, they earn time.
  • No ads, no tracking — We don't sell your family's data. The app is free.
  • Built by teens — Drift was built by two high schoolers who understand both sides of the screen time problem.

Get Started in 2 Minutes

Download Drift, create a parent account, and send your child a family code. They join on their phone, and you start setting tasks. That's it.

Common questions

  • How do I delete my account? Email us with the subject "Delete my account" from the email you signed up with. We process deletions within 7 days.
  • The app isn't blocking my apps. Make sure you've granted Screen Time permission when prompted. Go to Settings → Screen Time → Drift and ensure access is enabled.
  • My balance disappeared. Balances reset each day. Make sure your phone's timezone is set correctly (Settings → General → Date & Time → Set Automatically).
  • I can't sign in. Try resetting your password via the login screen. If that doesn't work, email us.

Response time

How It Works

Three steps to
taking control

01

Choose your blocks

Pick the apps that steal your time — TikTok, Instagram, X, whatever. Drift uses iOS Screen Time to lock them down.

02

Add tasks that matter to you. Work out, read, study, cook — or create your own. Each completed task earns screen time.

03

Earn your scroll
